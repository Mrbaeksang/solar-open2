# Publish two age-banded learning tracks

The textbook has an Easy Track for readers from grade 5 through grade 9 and a Standard Track for non-specialist readers from grade 10 onward. We choose two tracks over one elementary-to-middle-school edition because the reading levels and suitable examples across grades 1–9 differ too widely, while a third track for grades 1–4 would require a distinct visual, voice, and guardian-safety design that is outside the initial scope.
